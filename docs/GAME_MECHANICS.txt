MASTER OF MAGIC v1.31 - GAME MECHANICS, AS THE CODE COMPUTES THEM
================================================================
What the game actually does, topic by topic, read from the recovered source (src/), which compiles back into the
shipped v1.31 executables byte for byte - so every rule below is what 1.31 runs, not what the manual says. Each rule
names the function it comes from (FILE.C in src/wizards/ unless said otherwise): read the function for the details,
the edge cases and the bug notes. Written 2026-10-26.

How sure:
- The city calculations of section 2 (food, production, gold, building upkeep, research, power, rebels) were
  re-implemented and match the stored values of EVERY city in several real saves (28 / 28 and 30 / 30 cities).
- Everything else is read from the code (and the code is exact); the open question is only whether our reading
  of a variable's meaning is right. Many rules are also exercised by the regression cases of BUGS_AND_QUIRKS.txt.
- Where 1.31 does something wrong, the bug number is given (#n = BUGS_AND_QUIRKS.txt). Fixes by the community patch:
  COMMUNITY_PATCH_CROSSCHECK.txt.

Conventions: Random(n) is 1..n (not 0-based). Integer arithmetic truncates unless "rounded up" is said. "x" multiplies.
"Built" means standing OR replaced by its upgrade: a replaced building still gives its bonus (BLDSTATE_REPLACED). The
human is player 0, the neutral player 5. Difficulty 0..4 = Intro, Easy, Normal, Hard, Impossible.


1. THE MAP
----------
- Two planes (Arcanus, Myrror) of 60 x 40 squares; columns wrap east-west, rows do not. A square's terrain is a word;
  the type is the word modulo 762 (TERRAIN.C header); the single squares are 0xa2..0xb8, the rest blended variants.
- City area: the 5 x 5 square around the city without its four corners = 21 squares; corrupted squares (terrain flag
  0x20) are left out (City_Area_Tiles, CITYCALC.C). A square inside two or more city areas is SHARED and gives each
  city half of its food, production bonus and minerals (Tile_Is_Shared; the Terrain_* and Tile_* functions).
- Food per square, in half-food (Terrain_Food, TERRAIN.C): grassland 3, forest / hills 1, river 4, sorcery node 4,
  nature node 5, ocean 0, swamp 0 (#272; the manual says 1/2). A city's terrain food cap = the sum over its area / 2
  (the code sums quarter units and divides by 4), x 1.5 with Gaia's Blessing (City_Terrain_Food_Cap).
- Production bonus per square (Terrain_Production_Pct): mountains and the chaos node 5 %, hills and blended deserts
  3 % (the plain desert square 0 %: #490), forest and the nature node 3 % (6 % with Gaia's Blessing).
- Minerals (TERRAIN.C): gold - silver 2, gold ore 3, gems 5; power - mithril 1, adamantium 2, quork 3, crysx 5;
  unit cost - iron ore 5 %, coal 10 %. Dwarven cities double them; a Miners' Guild gives x 1.5 for gold, and doubles
  the unit-cost cut (#353: meant as +50 %). Mithril / adamantium in the area give the city's new units weapon grade
  2 / 3, an Alchemists' Guild grade 1 (City_Weapon_Grade). Wild game: +2 food per square (+1 if shared).
- Roads (Road_Build, CITYCALC.C): movement cost 1 for walking units; enchanted roads (all of Myrror's) cost 0.
  Turns to build (Road_Build_Turns): grassland 3, desert 4, river 5, forest / tundra / hills 6, swamp / mountain /
  volcano 8, nodes 4 / 5 / 8; divided among the engineers working (Stack_Move, MOVESTK.C).
- Terrain changes (Change Terrain, Raise Volcano, Gaia's Blessing, a cooling volcano) do not update the movement-cost
  maps, which only the world generator builds: the square keeps the old terrain's movement costs (#484, TERRCHG.C).
- Volcanoes: each owned volcano counts for its owner (power); each turn a volcano becomes a mountain with 2 %
  (Turn_Volcano_Decay, TURNUPD.C).
- A city cannot be founded on water, a tower, a node, an intact lair, or within distance 3 of a city
  (Tile_Settle_Blocker, OUTPOST.C). Continents never reach the map seam (MAPGEN_SURVEY.txt).


2. CITIES (CITYCALC.C; recomputed by City_Recalculate, ECONOMY.C)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Citizens are farmers, workers and rebels; workers = population - farmers - rebels.

FOOD (City_Calc_Food)
  farmers x 2 (x 3 for Halflings or with an Animists' Guild), +2 Foresters' Guild; halved by Famine;
  above the terrain food cap only half the excess counts: food = cap + (food - cap) / 2;
  then +2 Granary, +3 Farmers' Market, + wild game. Each citizen eats 1; each normal (not fantastic, not undead)
  non-hero unit costs 1 food upkeep (Wizard_Food_Upkeep, ECONOMY.C).
MAXIMUM POPULATION (City_Max_Population)
  the terrain food cap (halved by Famine) +2 Granary +3 Farmers' Market + wild game. At most 25 citizens.
GROWTH (City_Growth_Rate) - in growth points; 100 points = one more citizen (Turn_City_Growth, TURNUPD.C)
  starving (food below the population): (food - population) x 5 (negative); a maximum of 0: -population;
  otherwise (max - population + 1) / 2 + the race's growth (below) + Granary 2 + Farmers' Market 3; no growth at 25
  citizens; doubled by Stream of Life or a Population Boom; then x (100 + P) / 100 with P = -25 under Dark Rituals and,
  while producing Housing, +50 for a 1-citizen city or the worker percentage, + Sawmill 10, + Builders' Hall 15.
  Neutral cities grow at half the rate (none from 2 x (difficulty + 1) citizens); AI cities x the difficulty's growth %.
  Only an EXACT match with the maximum stops growth: a city above its maximum keeps growing (#103).
  Race growth (race_table, WIZARDS.C): Lizardmen +1, Barbarians +2, Beastmen 0, Halflings 0, High Men 0, Orcs 0,
  Draconians / Gnolls / Klackons / Nomads -1, Dark Elves / Dwarves / High Elves / Trolls -2.
PRODUCTION (City_Calc_Production)
  base = (workers x 2 (3 for Dwarves and Klackons) + farmers / 2, rounded up);
  x (100 + the area's terrain bonuses + Foresters' Guild 25 + Sawmill 25 + Miners' Guild 50 + Mechanicians' Guild 50
  + Inspirations 100) / 100; halved (rounded up) by Cursed Lands.
GOLD (City_Calc_Gold)
  taxed = (population - rebels) x tax rate / 2 (tax rate 0..6 = 0, 0.5 .. 3.0 gold per citizen; a neutral city 1
  each); doubled for Dwarves; + the area's mineral gold;
  x (100 + bonus %) / 100, bonus % = trade (min of: square 20 on a river, 10 beside water, 30 both + road trade
  (City_Road_Trade_Pct: the population of every road-connected city, half for the same race) + 50 for Nomads,
  3 x population) + Marketplace 50 + Bank 50 + Merchants' Guild 100 + Prosperity 100;
  + half the production when producing Trade Goods; at most 255. Odd populations lose the half gold.
BUILDING UPKEEP (City_Calc_Building_Upkeep, ECONOMY.C)
  the gold upkeep of every standing building (BUILDDAT.LBX).
RESEARCH (City_Calc_Research)
  Library 2, Sages' Guild 3, University 5, Wizards' Guild 8; none in neutral cities.
POWER (City_Calc_Power)
  religious = Shrine 1 + Temple 2 + Parthenon 3 + Cathedral 4, x 1.5 with Divine / Infernal Power, x 2 with Dark
  Rituals; + Alchemists' Guild 3, - Wizards' Guild 3; + population for Dark Elves, + population / 2 for High Elves,
  Beastmen, Draconians; + the wizard's spellbooks in the fortress city (+5 more on Myrror); + the area's mineral
  power. The moons: Good Moon adds the religious power for Life wizards and takes half for Death wizards, Bad Moon
  the reverse (floor 0 before the additions). 0 during the Mana Short event and in neutral cities.
UNREST (City_Rebels)
  unrest % = tax unrest (tax rate 0..6: 0, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 75) + the race table (TERRSTAT.LBX entry 1: the ruling
  wizard's home race against the city's race) x 10, + 25 under Famine;
  rebels = population x unrest % / 100, less the calming: Shrine / Temple / Parthenon / Cathedral 1 each (x 1.5 with
  Divine / Infernal Power; none under Evil Presence unless the owner has Death books), Oracle 2, Animists' Guild 1 -
  all REPLACED by -1 under another wizard's Great Wasting or -2 under Armageddon - then -1 Dark Rituals, -2 Pestilence,
  -1 Cursed Lands, +1 Just Cause, +2 Gaia's Blessing; when that is not enough, half the normal units in the city
  (martial law); 0 with Stream of Life; at most the population.
AI CHEATS (City_Recalculate + difficulty_table, WIZARDS.C) - an AI wizard's cities, in percent:
               growth  outpost  production  gold  power  research  food  building upkeep
  Intro         100     100      100        100    100    100      100    100
  Easy          100     100      125        125    125    100      125     90
  Normal        150     150      150        150    150    100      150     75
  Hard          200     200      200        200    200    110      200     60
  Impossible    400     400      400        400    400    150      400     30
  AI unit gold upkeep: 3 / 4 on Hard, 1 / 2 on Impossible (Unit_Gold_Upkeep, ECONOMY.C); an AI's unit food upkeep is
  scaled by the building-upkeep column (Wizard_Income_Summary). The power factor also scales an AI's node power
  (Wizard_Power_Base).
BUYING (City_Buy_Price, CITY.C): the remaining cost x 4 with no progress yet, x 3 below half the remaining cost
  (the actual threshold: progress < remaining / 2), x 2 from there. Selling a building returns cost / 3
  (City Walls / 6), once per turn per city (Building_Sell_Price).
UNIT COST: a unit's cost is cut by the area's iron / coal (at most 50 %; not for transports and some special units:
  City_Production_Cost).
OUTPOSTS (Outposts_Grow): a city of population 0 gains Random(3) tens of people when Random(100) <= the terrain
  food cap + the race's outpost growth + Gaia's Blessing 20 + Stream of Life 10 + minerals (#5: read at the wrong
  square), x the difficulty's outpost % (applied to the human too: #121); it shrinks by Random(2) on a 5 % chance
  (+5 Evil Presence, +10 each Pestilence / Famine / Chaos Rift); at 10 tens it becomes a hamlet, at 0 it is deserted.
CAPTURE / RAZE (City_Captured, COMBUTIL.C; Combat_End, COMBEND.C): the old owner loses fame equal to the city size;
  kept: the winner gains fame size - 2 and the plunder (City_Plunder_Gold: neutral city Random(10) per citizen,
  else the owner's gold x the city's share of the owner's population); razed: the winner loses fame (the size) and gets the
  plunder + a tenth of the cost of every building.


3. A WIZARD'S ECONOMY EACH TURN (ECONOMY.C, TURNUPD.C)
------------------------------------------------------
POWER BASE (Wizard_Power_Base): the owned nodes (a warped node -5; else its power x (magic setting + 1) / 2, halved /
  doubled by the Conjunctions, doubled by the realm's Mastery retort and again by Node Mastery) - for an AI scaled by
  the difficulty - + the cities' power + volcanoes. Nothing during Mana Short; 0 while casting Spell of Return.
SPLIT (Wizard_Magic_Split): the power base by the wizard's mana / skill / research percentages (research takes the
  rounding rest); Mana Focusing +25 % mana; research + the cities' research + Sage heroes (3 x (level + 1), Super Sage
  4.5 x); then + the research bonus % for the spell being researched (Research_Bonus_Pct): Sage Master 25, Conjurer
  25 for summons, the realm's Mastery 15 (Chaos, Nature, Sorcery), 10 per book of the realm above 7, Runemaster 25
  for Arcane.
INCOME (Turn_Apply_Magic_Income, Turn_Gold_Income): research points go to the spell, mana to the reserve, skill
  points to the casting skill (x 1.5 for Archmage); gold = the cities' gold - building upkeep + 10 per Noble hero +
  half the food surplus over the food upkeep. Gold and mana are capped at 30000 at the end of the turn.
CASTING SKILL (Wizard_Casting_Skill, WIZSTATS.C): the n where 2 + 4 + .. + 2n = n (n + 1) first reaches the skill
  points; Archmage +10. Heroes in the fortress city add half their spell skill (Fortress_Hero_Skill_Bonus).
UPKEEP: gold - each normal unit its type's upkeep (none for fantastic and undead units, Noble heroes, or a Famous
  wizard's heroes), less the effective fame (Wizard_Gold_Upkeep); unit types' upkeep = (cost - 1) / 50 + 1, the
  first spearmen of each race free (UnitTypes_Init_Upkeep_Sounds). Mana - fantastic units (x 1.5 when undead), 3 / 4
  for a Conjurer, + every unit enchantment, city enchantment and global enchantment the wizard maintains; halved for
  a Channeler (Wizard_Mana_Upkeep). Food - see section 2.
SHORTFALLS (Turn_Pay_Upkeep): a food shortage disbands normal units from the newest down; a gold shortage disbands
  units with gold upkeep (heroes too); a mana shortage removes, in up to four random-order rounds, unit enchantments,
  city enchantments, global enchantments and summoned units (Upkeep_Shortfall_*).
FAME (Wizard_Effective_Fame): fame + Legendary heroes + Just Cause; it lowers gold upkeep and drives merchants,
  heroes, mercenaries and the score.


4. THE ORDER OF A TURN (Next_Turn, NEXTTURN.C)
----------------------------------------------
The cities recomputed; the AI's obsolete units disbanded; the AI turn (AI_Overland_Turn, AITURN.C); then, unless a
Time Stop runs: gold, power, magic income and research; the random events; upkeep; outpost and city growth (with
production); the Grand Vizier's picks; the offers (merchants, mercenaries, heroes); Nightshade; diplomacy; volcanoes.
Under Time Stop its caster pays 200 mana a turn (it ends at 0). Then the overland casting, the unit table,
experience, healing, the power statistics and the music mood; the turn counter, the caps, the autosave (every 4th
turn) and the random seed.


5. MAGIC
--------
RESEARCH (TREASURE.C): 8 research candidates, refilled from the pool of researchable spells - per realm those of
  the lowest rarity tier that still has any, plus Arcane - and the Spell of Mastery once the pool is empty; sorted
  by research turns (Research_Candidates_Refill, Research_Pool_Build). Learning a spell lowers the Spell of
  Mastery's cost by half the spell's research cost (Wizard_Learn_Spell, TURNUPD.C). A new spellbook makes random
  unknown spells of its realm researchable (Wizard_Gain_Spellbook; #238 above 10 books).
CASTING COST (Spell_Casting_Cost, WIZSTATS.C): the base cost, x 1.5 for Nature and Life spells while anyone has Evil
  Omens, x 5 for a combat spell cast overland, less the reduction % (Spell_Cost_Reduction_Pct): 10 per book of the
  realm above 7, the realm's Mastery 15, Artificer 50 for item creation, Conjurer 25 for summons, Runemaster 25 for
  Arcane.
OVERLAND CASTING (Turn_Overland_Casting, TURNUPD.C): each turn the spell in progress gets min(mana, the skill left,
  the cost left); if the rest then fits in min(mana, skill) it is finished at once, borrowing from next turn's skill.
COMBAT CASTING (COMBSPL.C): the cost x the range multiplier (Combat_Range_Multiplier, by the distance from the
  wizard's fortress): x 0.5 at the fortress, x 1.0 up to 5 squares, then +0.5 beyond 5, 10, 15 and 20 squares (x 3.0;
  also on the other plane); a Channeler pays x 1.0 everywhere (x 0.5 at the fortress). Paid from the casting skill
  left this turn and mana. Counter Magic and node auras may make a spell fizzle (Combat_Spell_Action).
DISPELLING (Dispel_Roll, SPELLFX.C; Disenchant_Area_Overland, OVERCAST.C): a dispel of strength S removes a spell of
  resistance R when Random(250) <= 250 x S / (S + R); R = the spell's cost (x 5 for unit enchantments), x 2 with the
  caster's realm-mastery retort, x 3 with Archmage too (Spell_Dispel_Resistance). Suppress Magic, Tranquility (chaos
  spells) and Life Force (death spells) counter a spell when Random(250) <= 125000 / (R + 500) (Overland_Cast_Spell).
  Nightshade dispels foreign city enchantments: Random(250) <= 25000 n / (R + 100 n), n = Nightshade squares.
CITY PROTECTION (City_Spell_Protection_Check, OVERSPL2.C): Consecration blocks Chaos and Death spells on the city, a
  realm Ward its realm, Nightshade may dispel. Wards also stop that realm's creatures from entering (City_Ward_Check).
SPELL EFFECTS: each spell's function is named after it - OVERCAST.C (dispatch by effect type), OVERSPEL.C,
  OVERSPL2.C, OVERSPL3.C (overland), COMBDISP.C, SPELLFX.C, COMBSPL.C (combat). A few worth knowing:
  Drain Power drains ten Random(20) (10..200) mana; Cruel Unminding cuts the skill by Random(10) % (at least 1);
  Great Wasting / Armageddon corrupt / raise 4..6 squares a turn, 3+ squares from the caster's cities; Meteor Storm hits
  every unit outside a city with Call Chaos' damage (not its own) and city buildings at 1 %; Call the Void / Earthquake
  / Nature's Wrath destroy buildings at 50 / 15 / 5 %.


6. UNITS AND HEROES
-------------------
EXPERIENCE (Unit_Calc_Level, ECONOMY.C; exp_level_thresholds, WIZARDS.C): levels at 0, 20, 60, 120 experience
  (normal units stop at level 3 = 120), heroes on at 200, 300, 450, 600, 1000 (level 8). Every normal living unit
  gains 1 experience a turn (Turn_Unit_Experience). Heroism = level 3 at least; Warlord +1 level; Crusade +1 level.
  Armsmaster heroes add 2 x (level + 1) experience a turn to the non-heroes of their stack (Super: 3 x).
HIT POINTS (Unit_Max_Hits, UNITSTAT.C): the type's per figure, Black Channels +1, Lionheart +3; a hero +1 at levels
  1, 2 and 4 and its Constitution; every unit +1 at levels 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8; Charm of Life +25 %.
STATISTICS (Battle_Unit_Init, Unit_Apply_Modifiers, Battle_Unit_Combat_Modifiers, UNITSTAT.C): the type's values,
  then experience, hero abilities and items, weapon grade, enchantments, global and combat enchantments, node auras
  and curses - each function lists its modifiers.
MOVES (Unit_Move_Points, WIZSTATS.C): the type's moves, at least 3 (6 half-moves) with flight, +1 with Endurance, +
  item bonuses; a ship x 1.5 under its owner's Wind Mastery, x 0.5 against more enemy Wind Masteries. A stack moves at
  its slowest unit unless a transport or Wind Walker carries it (Stack_Moves_Available, MOVECHK.C). Movement modes
  (UNITMOVE.C): a mode counts for the stack when every unit has it or one unit carries the others (Pathfinding,
  Wind Walking, ships).
STACKS: at most 9 units on a square; a tenth makes the cheapest leave (Stack_Limit_Enforce).
HEALING (Turn_Heal_Units, TURNUPD.C): damage heals by total hits / divisor a turn, the divisor 20 in the field, 10 in
  a city, 6 with an Animists' Guild; a Healer heals its stack by a fifth. Not undead or race 20 units.
HIRING (HIRE.C, TURNUPD.C): a hero costs its price + a quarter per level (half for Charismatic); a rejected hero for
  hire returns one level higher. Offers each turn (Turn_Random_Offers): a merchant (human only) with fame / 25 + 2 %
  (x 2 Famous, at most 10 %) at 3 x the item's cost; mercenaries fame / 20 + 1 %; a hero fame / 25 + 3 % (divided by
  the heroes already hired). #3: the AI wizards' chances use the HUMAN's fame.


7. COMBAT
---------
ROLLS (COMBRES.C): each point of attack hits on Random(10) >= 8 - to-hit bonus, always on a 10 (Roll_To_Hit) - 30 %
  base; each shield (defense point) blocks on Random(10) >= 8 - block bonus (Roll_Blocks) - 30 % base; a resistance
  roll fails by Random(10) - (resistance + bonus + modifier) when the roll is higher (Roll_Resist).
AN ATTACK (Attack_Resolve, Combat_Exchange): ranged - one shot (Haste: two); melee - the attacker's thrown / breath /
  gaze attack first, a defender's gaze, Wall of Fire, then First Strike strikes before the counter-attack, otherwise
  blow and counter are simultaneous. Figure by figure: special touches (Stoning, Death, Life Steal, Destruction,
  Dispel Evil), the to-hit roll, Blur, the blocks per defending figure (Invulnerability -2 damage). To-hit penalties:
  missiles -1 per 3 squares of distance (Long Range: at most -1), -1 against an invisible target; a counter-attack
  loses times-attacked / 2. Damage comes in three kinds: normal, drain (Life Steal, Create Undead) and irrecoverable.
SHIELDS (Defender_Shields): Large Shield +2 against ranged; immunities make 50 shields (Weapon Immunity at least 10);
  Bless +3 and Righteousness 50 against Chaos / Death; Elemental Armor +10 or Resist Elements +3 against Nature /
  Chaos ranged attacks; armour piercing halves them.
RESISTANCE (Resist_Bonus): Charmed hero +30, Magic Immunity +30, Righteousness +30 (chaos, death), Elemental Armor
  +10 / Resist Elements +3 (nature, chaos), Bless +3 (chaos, death), Resist Magic +5.
ACTIONS (Combat_Unit_Attack): a shot ends the unit's movement (20 half moves) and costs 1 ammunition, or 3 spell
  points for heroes and casters with magic shots; a melee attack costs half the unit's moves. Adjacent units shoot only when their ranged strength is above half
  their melee. Wall Crusher breaches a wall square on 25 % at range, 50 % adjacent.
EACH COMBAT TURN (Combat_Turn_Start, COMBMOVE.C): Terror, Entangle (-2 moves), Web, Black Sleep, Confusion
  (Random(4): no move, a random move, or changing sides), Regeneration; Call Lightning, Wrack, Mana Leak and the
  vortices act.
RETREAT (Combat_Retreat_Resolve, COMBTURN.C): a losing side's fled units die 1 in 2 (heroes 1 in 4; none of the
  current player's at Intro / Easy); held, confused or webbed ones always.
STRATEGIC (auto) COMBAT (Combat_Strategic, COMBAUTO.C): per side totals of toughness (hits x 3, scaled by defense),
  missile and attack values (AI_*_Value), x 1.5 for non-neutral sides when no human fights; the wizards' magic adds
  to them; up to 3 missile rounds, then melee rounds, each doing (total + magic) x Random(10) / 100 against the
  other side's toughness. The attacker wins if its attack is over 10, else the defender if its is. The losers all die;
  the winner loses the share of its hits that its toughness lost. Mixing of realm effects: #182.
AFTER A BATTLE (Combat_End, COMBEND.C): dead heroes' items go to the winner (HEROITEM.C: the human picks, an AI
  re-equips and sells the rest for half their cost in mana); Create Undead / Zombie Mastery raise the dead; fame and
  relations change; a city taken may be razed.


8. DIPLOMACY (DIPLOACT.C, DIPLOTRN.C, AIDIPLO.C, DIPLOMSG.C)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Each wizard has toward each other a relation (-100..100), a base relation it drifts back to, and treaty / trade
  / peace interests that regrow by 10 a turn below 100 (Diplo_Interest_Regrow) and drift (Diplo_Interest_Drift).
- Drift each turn (Diplo_Relation_Drift): treaty bonuses; army envy (-10 on a 1 in 20 while the human's army is
  below twice the other's); the human's city count above (land size + 1) x 3 angers everyone; the pull toward the
  base relation.
- Peace (Diplo_Make_Peace): relation +40, a peace timer of 8..13 turns, mutual city curses removed.
- Breaking a treaty or declaring war costs relation with the third parties (Diplo_Break_Treaty, Diplo_Declare_War).
- AI war decisions (AI_War_Check): never before turn 100 against the human; a Chaotic AI on Random(300) <= difficulty;
  otherwise relation + an army term + personality + bias <= -150, rolled 1 in 20. Units parked within 2 squares of an
  ally's city cost the treaty on the third consecutive check (AI_Border_Tension).
- Proposals (AI_Diplo_Propose_To_Human): a pact when the relation is above 15 and a roll succeeds, an alliance above
  50, else a spell exchange. AI-to-AI diplomacy (AI_Diplo_Turn_AI_Vs_AI) rolls alliances, trades and peace each turn.
- Overland actions change relations: hostile city spells -5..-50 (Hostile_Spell_Relations), global enchantments
  anger the other wizards each turn (Global_Enchant_Relations), attacks set hostility (Diplo_After_Attack).


9. THE AI (AITURN.C, AIPROD.C, AINEUTRL.C, AIUNITS.C, AISPELL.C)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- It plans with the whole map: the AI's own modules never read the exploration map (only the human has one; the
  save holds no AI fog) and do not test invisibility themselves.
- Tax (AI_Set_Tax): up while the gold income is below 75 per 50 turns played, down above 200, at most 2.0 gold.
- Gold and mana (AI_Balance_Gold_Mana): moves gold to mana and back by fixed ratios; with Alchemy re-splits them
  90 / 10 before turn 150, then 30 / 70.
- Power split (AI_Magic_Allocation): re-chosen every 16..25 turns from a 10 / 30 / 10 base plus five +10 picks by
  its objective; before turn 30 35 / 40 / 25 (research / mana / skill); casting the Spell of Mastery 0 / 90 / 10.
- Strategy (AI_Choose_Strategy) and hostility (AI_Hostility_Replan) re-planned every 16..25 turns; personalities
  (Maniacal, Ruthless, Aggressive, Chaotic, Lawful, Peaceful) and objectives weight the choices.
- City production (AI_Wizard_Choose_Production): a garrison checklist (Barracks, Builders' Hall, Shrine, Smithy,
  Granary), a Trade Goods roll, then a weighted pick: buildings by category and objective, units by (cost / 10)^2,
  settlers / engineers / transports by rules. It buys when the buy price x a random factor is below its gold
  (AI_City_Maybe_Buy). Mercenaries: never before turn 50, then at most half its gold (AI_Accept_Mercenary_Offer).
- The neutral player (AINEUTRL.C): lairs release roaming monsters and neutral cities send raiders on timers that grow
  with difficulty (from turn 50); neutral stacks attack the nearest weak wizard city or are disbanded.
- From turn 100 the AI disbands units worth less than half its average unit cost (AI_Disband_Obsolete_Units).


10. RANDOM EVENTS (EVENTGEN.C, EVENTS.C)
----------------------------------------
- Chance each turn (Event_Roll): the turns since the last event (none for 5 turns after one), x 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 4/5
  by difficulty; an event fires when Random(512) <= chance. Up to 5 tries of a random event; the victim is weighted by
  power + research + army (good events favour the weak: Event_Pick_Wizard). Many conditions reject an event (Pirates
  under 100 gold, Meteor / Marriage before turn 150, Rebellion in the fortress city ...).
- Events: Meteor, Gift, Disjunction, Marriage, Earthquake, Pirates (30..50 % of the gold), Plague, Rebellion,
  Donation (100 + 5 x Random(100) gold), Depletion / New Minerals (never fire: #1), Population Boom, and the six
  global ones (Good / Bad Moon, the three Conjunctions, Mana Short). Lasting events end on Random(20) <= turns - 2 after
  4 turns (Events_Process).


11. LAIRS AND TREASURE (LAIR.C)
-------------------------------
- Guardians are fought as a normal battle (Lair_Attack, ATTACK.C). The treasure (Treasure_Roll): gold, mana, prefab
  items (by the lair's item values), a spell of a rarity, spellbooks or retorts (3 in 4 a book of the lair's realm:
  Lair_Book_Realm), a prisoner hero when a hero slot is free.


12. THE END OF THE GAME (ENDGAME.C, MASTERY.C, FINSCORE.C)
----------------------------------------------------------
- A captured fortress (Wizard_Fortress_Captured): a wizard with 2+ cities is banished and starts casting the Spell of
  Return (Wizard_Banish; an AI with skill under 40 or no mana is eliminated instead); otherwise eliminated - its
  cities go neutral, its units die or go neutral, the winner takes half its gold and mana and gains 5 fame.
- The game is won when no rival has a fortress or a city (All_Rivals_Defeated), or by completing the Spell of Mastery
  (Spell_Of_Mastery_Complete): the game ends at once for everyone.
- SCORE (FINSCORE.C): known spells + population / 2 (in thousands) + 50 per banished wizard + 2 x effective fame +
  (2000 - 2 x turn, if the fortress stands and nobody else cast Mastery) + 250 for the Spell of Mastery, x 1/2, 3/4,
  1, 2, 3 by difficulty. The turn bonus has no floor (#109).
