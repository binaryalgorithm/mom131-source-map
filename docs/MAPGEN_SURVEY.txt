WORLD GENERATOR SURVEY - how close does land come to the x = 59 / 0 map seam?
============================================================================
Run 2026-10-17; published 2026-10-26.

WHY: the 60 x 40 map wraps east-west (column 59 is next to column 0), and several bugs only happen at that seam:
#27 (cities' shared-area bitmap does not wrap), #131 (terrain re-tiling at the seam), #203 (keypad movement across the
seam goes one row off). Whether they can matter in play depends on whether land - and so cities and units on land -
ever reaches the seam.

METHOD: 30 new games generated by the real MAGIC.EXE v1.31 under DOSBox-X, driven by a script (10 per land size;
wizard Merlin, Barbarians, all other options default), each world read from the save the generator writes
(SAVE9.GAM, layout in SAVE_LAYOUT.txt). For comparison, 9 more v1.31 saves of other games (4 older saves and the 5 in
the ReMoM repository). Per plane: the land squares on rows 2-37, the nearest one's distance to the seam
(min(x, 59 - x)), how many lie at distance 0 / 1 / 2 / 3, and the land squares on the seam columns of the polar rows
1 / 38 ("polar seam").

RESULT - nearest land to the seam, rows 2-37, by land size (planes counted; distance: number of planes)
  small    20 planes   2: 1   3: 8   4: 5   5: 4   6: 2
  medium   20 planes   2: 2   3: 8   4: 5   5: 4   6: 1
  large    20 planes   2: 14  3: 3   4: 3
  older    18 planes   2: 11  3: 5   4: 1   5: 1
On all 78 planes, no land square on rows 2-37 lies in columns 58, 59, 0 or 1. Land squares at distance 2 are common on
large maps (up to 9 per plane); at distance 3, up to 10. The seam columns hold land only on the polar rows: rows 0 / 39
are all tundra and cannot be entered, and rows 1 / 38 have tundra at the seam on most maps (0 - 4 squares per plane).

WHY (the source): Gen_Landmasses (src/magic/WORLDGEN.C) raises land by random walks that turn back at the map edge -
x 2 .. 57, y 4 .. 35 - so a continent's own squares never reach columns 0-1 or 58-59. The polar land comes from
Gen_Height_To_Terrain: rows 0 / 39 all tundra, plus random tundra runs of 1 .. 4 squares on rows 1 / 38.

CONSEQUENCE for the seam bugs (their entries in BUGS_AND_QUIRKS.txt give the full reach): #27 needs a city at x 58..1,
which is possible only on the polar tundra of rows 1 / 38; #131 needs terrain changed on a polar seam square or
terraforming that reaches columns 59 / 0; #203 needs a unit crossing the seam - ships, swimmers, flyers, or walkers on
the polar tundra.

Per-map detail (plane 0 = Arcanus, 1 = Myrror; "land" = land squares on rows 2-37; "d0..d3" = land squares at
distance 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 from the seam; "polar" = land on the seam columns of rows 1 / 38)
